Oddsketch privacy policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Oddsketch is a drawing game for players aged 13 and over. This page says what the game keeps, where it keeps it, and who sees it.

Everything stays on your phone

We don't collect anything.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your progress: scores, best runs, ink, and the pens, colors and papers you own.
  • Your drawings in the Sketchbook.
  • A small play log, used to improve the game: when a run starts and ends, how long it lasts, your score, how long your first counted drawing took, the gap between runs and the ink you earned.

What the game does not do

  • It doesn't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • It doesn't send your drawings, progress or play log anywhere.
  • It has no ads and no analytics.

Reading your drawings

The game reads your drawings on the phone itself. Your drawings never leave the phone to be read.

Sharing

You can share a drawing or a score. This happens only when you tap Share. It opens your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. What happens next is up to the app you pick.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game. That removes everything it kept.

Children

Oddsketch is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date at the top. We will update it before the game adds ads, analytics or purchases.
