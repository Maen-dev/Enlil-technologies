Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. Uninstalling the game or changing phones removes it.

How do I ask you to delete my data?

The game keeps everything on your phone, so uninstalling it deletes it. See the privacy policy: https://enliltechnologies.com/oddsketch/privacy#delete
