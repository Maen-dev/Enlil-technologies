Oddsketch privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Oddsketch is a drawing game for players aged 13 and over. This page says what the game keeps, what it shares for ads, and who sees it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress: scores, best runs, ink, and the pens, colors and papers you own.
  • Your drawings in the Sketchbook.
  • A small play log, used to improve the game: when a run starts and ends, how long it lasts, your score and the ink you earned.
  • The game reads your drawings on the phone itself. Your drawings never leave the phone.

We don't run our own servers for the game, and we don't collect your name, email or any account.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Appodeal, which picks each ad from several ad networks (for example AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff/Vungle, ironSource, Mintegral, InMobi, BidMachine, Digital Turbine, Bigo Ads and Amazon).

  • You see a short video only when you choose to watch one for extra time, and from your third game, a full-screen ad between some games. Never while you draw.
  • To show and measure ads, Appodeal and those networks collect: your phone's advertising ID (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and rough location from it, the kind of phone and its settings, your network, and which ads you saw or tapped, plus technical data about how the ads loaded.
  • They use it to show ads, count views and taps, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, choose ads that suit you.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the game may track you. If you say no, no ad network gets your advertising ID, and the game asks for ads that aren't based on you. You still see ads, just not personalized ones.
  • Appodeal's privacy policy: https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy (it lists its ad partners and links to each one's policy).

Sharing

You can share a drawing or a score. This happens only when you tap Share. It opens your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

Your choices

  • To stop personalized ads on iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, and turn off Oddsketch (or "Allow Apps to Request to Track").
  • On Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads, and delete your advertising ID.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the game. That removes everything it kept on your phone.
  • We don't hold any data about you ourselves. Data the ad networks collected is kept under their own policies; you can ask them to delete it through the links in Appodeal's policy, or email us with the App ID shown on the game's About screen and we will pass the request on.

Children

Oddsketch is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date at the top.
